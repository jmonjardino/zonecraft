# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy, respecting differing opinions and experiences, accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling or insulting comments, harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions or comments that they deem inappropriate. Reports should be made privately to the maintainer using the contact method published by the repository host. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.
